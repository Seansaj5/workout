    Face & Neck

    Every single day, rest days included. About eight minutes in the mirror.

      Straps

      Gold chip means bring them.

      • Top sets only. Warm up bare-handed, strap the heaviest one or two sets. If you strap everything, your grip never gets the message.
      • Never on curls or carries. Farmer's carries and pinch holds exist to build the grip. Strapping them defeats the whole point.
      • The conflict is real. You now train forearms directly, so grip work always goes last in the session. Fry your hands early and the rows suffer.

      How this got harder

      • Three working sets, not two. This is the actual change. Grinding harder on two sets is not a substitute for more hard sets.
      • One intensity technique per exercise, last set only. Orange chips mark them. On every set it just wrecks recovery.
      • Deload every seventh week. Two sets, about 60 percent of your weights, one week. Six days at this intensity catches up around week eight.
      • Log the numbers. Beating last week is the whole mechanism, and you cannot beat what you did not write down.
      • Eat for it. More volume without more food makes you tired, not bigger.
      Home gym: magnetic cable unit, 200lb stack, two barbells, dumbbells to 75, rack with pull-up bar, bench. Each cue names the pulley height. Set marks clear automatically each morning.
