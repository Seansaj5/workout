      Straps

      Gold chip means bring them.

      • Top sets only. Warm up bare-handed and strap the heaviest one or two sets. Strap everything and your grip never gets the message.
      • Never on carries. Farmer's carries exist to build grip. Strapping them defeats the point entirely.
      • Grip work goes last. You train forearms directly now, so fry your hands early and every row after it suffers.

      Rules

      • Two working sets, both all-out. Every set goes to the last clean rep. Two is enough only because neither one is held back. On barbell presses and squats, stop one rep short of a miss, nobody is spotting you.
      • One technique per exercise, last set only. Orange chips mark them. Everywhere at once just wrecks recovery.
      • Deload week seven. One set, around 60 percent, one week. Six days at this intensity catches up around week eight.
      • Log the numbers. Beating last week is the entire mechanism, and you cannot beat what you did not write down.
      • Eat for it. More volume without more food makes you tired, not bigger.
